Our buyer's guide covers what GoHighLevel is, what its three plans cost in dollars and when it is the wrong purchase. This post is about the lines below the plan — the ones a UK business only meets on the first invoice — and about the one honest way to turn a dollar price list into a pound budget.

<Callout variant="note" title="TL;DR">
  The plan is a dollar figure, and so is everything under it: texts by the segment, calls by the
  minute, email by the thousand, WhatsApp by the message plus a monthly channel fee, AI by the plan
  or by the minute, a UK number by the month with paperwork attached. Convert with a band from a
  dated twelve-month exchange-rate range, never a single rate, and budget the band's top. If an
  agency hosts you, ask which of these lines it passes through and at what mark-up.
</Callout>

## What the pricing page shows, and in which currency

Three plans — $97, $297 and $497 a month on the pricing page as read on 23 September 2026 — priced in US dollars with no pound option. The [buyer's guide](/blog/what-is-gohighlevel-2026-buyers-guide) walks through what each includes and which fits; this post starts where it stops.

## The lines below the plan

**Texts.** Billed per segment: 160 characters in the basic GSM-7 set, 70 if a single emoji or curly quote pushes the message into UCS-2, and fewer per part once a message runs to two, because each part carries a concatenation header (the platform's guide gives 67 for UCS-2 and no GSM-7 figure). The platform publishes non-US rates as country files attached to its phone-system pricing guide (1 September 2026) rather than as a table on the page, and points at Twilio's list prices as the reference: for the UK, $0.056 per outbound segment and $0.0075 inbound as at July 2026. Treat those as the proxy until you have read the UK file itself.

**Calls.** Per minute, inbound and outbound, from the same country file; Twilio's UK list has inbound calls to a local or mobile number at $0.0100 per minute as at August 2026. A receptionist plan that answers 300 minutes a month is 300 of these plus the AI line below.

**Email.** "$0.675 per 1,000" sent, on every plan, per the platform's billing guide of 22 August 2026.

**WhatsApp.** "$10 / month per sub-account" for the channel, then per message by category: for the UK, "$0.0667" marketing and "$0.0231" utility, marked "Updated Jul 2026". When an agency rebills them, the platform's default is "1.05X" and "A 5% Stripe fee" on top.

**AI.** The AI Employee is "$50 / month" or "$97 / month" per sub-account, or pay-per-use with per-minute and per-message rates shown inside the account. An agency can rebill AI usage to its sub-accounts only on the $497 plan, which is why a hosted receptionist plan on a smaller agency licence has to bundle its minutes rather than meter them.

**A UK number.** Rented by the month — Twilio's UK list shows a local number at $3.50 on its voice page and $1.15 on its SMS page, a mobile number at $2.50 — and registered on a regulatory record with the business name, registration and a UK address; a business without a UK registration uses a mobile or freephone number instead. A divert from the number you already publish avoids most of this line.

**Add-ons you may not need.** Listings sync at $30 and the SEO add-on at $79 per sub-account per month, each worth a question before it is switched on.

## "GoHighLevel SMS pricing UK" — the three things people search for

What a text costs (the segment rate above, from the UK file the platform attaches to its guide); why a short message sometimes costs double (the character set); and whether a branded sender name is cheaper (it is one-way only on the platform's UK guidance, so anything that expects a reply comes from a real number). The cheapest text is one segment, plain characters, from a number you already own.

## The AI lines, and the rebilling rule

Two ways to pay: the flat monthly AI plan per sub-account, or pay-per-use at rates the platform shows in the account. The rule that shapes a hosted plan is on the agency side: usage can be rebilled to a sub-account only on the $497 plan, per the platform's AI Employee article as at 14 September 2026. An agency on a smaller licence cannot pass your minutes through at cost; it has to bundle an allowance into its fee and publish an overage rate in pounds. Ask which it does.

## Converting honestly: a band, not a rate

A dollar price list converted at today's rate is a budget that is wrong tomorrow. The honest method is a band: take the highest and lowest sterling-dollar rates over the last twelve months from the Bank of England's published series, dated, and show every dollar line at both ends. Budget the top of the band, and never publish the midpoint as if it were a price. That is how the pound figures on our own plans are built, and it is why a usage schedule in pounds has to be reviewed each quarter rather than fixed for a year.

## A worked example, in units

Suppose a single-location business in its own account sends 100 missed-call texts and takes 60 replies in a month, sends 120 review requests by text, uses 300 receptionist minutes, 2,000 emails and 200 WhatsApp utility messages, and rents one UK number. In dollars at the list prices above that is 220 outbound segments, 60 inbound, 300 inbound minutes plus the AI line, 2,000 emails at the per-thousand rate, 200 utility messages plus the channel fee, and one rental — each a line you can price from the tables in your account. In pounds it is that total at the low and the high end of the dated Bank of England band, and the figure to budget is the high end. We leave the pound figures out of this post on purpose: the moment they are printed they are stale, and the band is the method.

## A checklist before you sign anything priced in dollars

1. You have read the platform's UK rate file itself, not a proxy.
2. You know the segment rule and your templates fit one segment.
3. You know whether AI is a plan or metered, and who can rebill it.
4. You know what the WhatsApp channel and messages cost, and the mark-up if rebilled.
5. The number is on a divert, or its rental and registration are on the list.
6. Your pound budget is a band from a dated twelve-month range, budgeted at the top.
7. If an agency hosts you, every pass-through line and its mark-up is written down.
