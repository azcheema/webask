### AJ.0 Correction carried into the existing drafts (found while outlining AJ.6, 2026-09-24)

App. V ("Month one is the consent audit" and "Database reactivation done lawfully"), App. Z.4 Q3
and App. AG.2's Email & SMS "Build" cell all say that records with no lawful basis get **one
electronic message asking whether they would like to hear from you**. Under PECR that message is
itself direct marketing to an individual subscriber without consent — the ICO's guidance says a
message asking for consent to marketing is marketing (confirmed 2026-09-24 on S03, under "What are direct
marketing purposes?": the list includes "contacting people to ask them for consent to direct
marketing", with the example of a hotel emailing previous guests to ask whether they would like to
consent to offers — "the hotel is still sending it for direct marketing purposes"). **The rule for every draft:** parked records are not emailed or
texted to ask for consent. Lawful routes are post (outside PECR's electronic-mail rule; UK GDPR
legitimate interests), a live call that clears the TPS screen (reg 21), or asking at the next visit
or purchase — or leaving them parked. Corporate subscribers may be emailed. The three passages were
rewritten on 2026-09-24 (App. V "Month one" and "Database reactivation" paragraphs; Z.4 Q3; AG.2's
Email & SMS "Build" cell), R53 records the rule, and App. V's pass-1 self-check line about R09 and
"often" is superseded by this note. S8 still reads the rewritten sentences with fresh eyes.
