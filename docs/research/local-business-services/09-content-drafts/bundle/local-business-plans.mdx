## Hosted or owned — your choice

Everything else we build lives in an account you own: your site on your hosting, your CRM under your own subscription, your data where you can reach it without us. That stays the default, and the [CRM page](/services/crm-automation) is where it is built.

These plans are the labelled exception. The phone, the follow-up and the reviews run in a sub-account under our agency licence, so there is no platform for you to learn, no subscription to manage and no configuration to inherit. The trade is control for convenience, and a trade like that is only fair if the terms are written down: where the data sits, who answers for it, what usage costs and in what currency, and exactly what leaves with you if you go. This page is those terms.

## The three plans

**Answer.** A hosted sub-account with a UK number registered to your business, or a divert from the one you publish. Every missed call texted back in seconds, as a service message. One inbox for those replies, your website chat and, if you want it, WhatsApp. A booking calendar with reminders. The mobile app for whoever holds the phone. A monthly report.

**Reputation.** Everything in Answer, plus the review programme — every customer asked, nobody incentivised, nothing hidden, replies drafted for your approval — and your Google Business Profile kept up: posts, questions, photos, and listings kept consistent by hand, with a sync tool as its own line at cost where several locations make it worth it.

**Follow-Up.** Everything in Reputation, plus two campaigns a month written and sent to the people you may lawfully message, one automation built or reviewed each month, a reactivation campaign each quarter on the records that can carry it, and a landing page a quarter for whatever you are promoting.

**The AI Receptionist module** sits on any plan: a narrow inbound agent on the same divert, disclosed as automated in its first sentence, with minutes at a published rate.

Each part is also a service of its own — [missed-call text-back](/services/missed-call-text-back), [review management](/services/review-management), [Google Business Profile management](/services/google-business-profile), [email and SMS marketing](/services/email-sms-marketing), [landing pages](/services/landing-pages) and the [AI receptionist](/services/ai-receptionist) — and each of those pages explains its own rules. The plans bundle the running of them into one monthly fee and one inbox.

## Where your data sits, and who is responsible for it

You stay the controller of your customers' data; we process it for you under a written contract, as UK GDPR Article 28 requires, with HighLevel, Inc. as our sub-processor. The platform is hosted in the United States. The transfer rests on HighLevel's certification, as at 23 September 2026, under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, with the UK Addendum to the standard contractual clauses as the fallback — both sit in HighLevel's own data processing agreement, and our contract with you mirrors it. If HighLevel changes a sub-processor, we get thirty days' notice and a right to object, and our contract passes both on to you.

We will not tell you the data stays in the UK, because it does not. What we will tell you is who holds it, under which contract, and how it comes back.

## What usage costs, and in which currency

The platform bills messages, calls and AI minutes in US dollars, and it bills WhatsApp per message. You pay for usage at cost, at the pound rates on our published schedule; we reset that schedule each quarter against the exchange rate. Each plan carries an allowance; beyond it you pay the schedule, nothing more. The plan fee itself is fixed in pounds for twelve months, so the only figure that moves with the exchange rate is the one that moves with what you use.

## Your number, your inbox, your calendar

A UK local number is registered to your business — your Companies House details go on the platform's regulatory record, and a business without a UK registration gets a UK mobile or freephone number instead — or the number you already publish is diverted, which is the option we recommend. Branded sender names only work one way, so anything that expects a reply comes from a real number. The inbox and the calendar live in your sub-account; the people who answer are yours, and the conversation history stays with the contact.

## If you leave

You get an export of your contacts, conversations and forms on request. The sub-account can be transferred to an agency account of your own: contacts with their history, websites, funnels, calendars and users move with it; automations arrive as drafts for you to switch on; the phone number moves where both sides use the platform's own telephony; channels such as WhatsApp, Google and Facebook are reconnected on your side; add-on subscriptions end with the plan and are set up again by you if you want them. It is a documented move with a checklist, not a seamless one, and the checklist is written at set-up so that leaving is never the first time anyone reads it. If the plan runs on a domain we assigned rather than one you own, that domain does not move with the sub-account, so the checklist names the domain and where its links point.

## What each plan reports every month

Answer: calls missed, texts sent, conversations that replied, bookings that followed. Reputation adds requests sent, reviews received, rating movement and reviews still unanswered, plus your profile's own insights. Follow-Up adds replies and bookings from each campaign. Counts from the account that runs it, which you can log into — nothing modelled, no percentage we did not measure.

## Clinics and practices: why patient data stays in your own account

If your customers are patients, the records you hold are health data, and a hosted sub-account on a US-hosted platform is the wrong place for them. Clinics and dental practices get the same automations built in an account they own, with the consent design and the advertising rules those pages set out — see [aesthetic clinics](/industries/aesthetic-clinics) and [dental practices](/industries/dental-practices). The plans on this page are for businesses whose contact records are names, numbers and jobs.

## What the plans cost

Each plan has a set-up fee per module and a monthly fee, shown on the [pricing page](/pricing) with the rest of our services and marked "+ VAT where applicable" until our VAT position is settled. Set-up is paid on signature, the plan monthly in advance and usage in arrears; there is no minimum term, and thirty days' notice ends it. What moves the price: the tier, a new number instead of a divert, the AI module, and usage.

## What is not in any plan

A website — bring the one you have, or see [web development](/services/web-development). Paid ads and social posting: the account has the tools; the plans do not use them. Outbound calls of any kind. And anything that asks the platform to send a promotion to a number without consent — the plans are built so that click is not available.

None of this is legal advice. It describes how the account is set up, who holds the data and what leaves with you; what you choose to send from it is yours to answer for.

We work remotely, with no UK office, which is why the configuration is written down as it is made and every setting in your sub-account is yours to inspect.
