You know the phone, the reviews and the follow-up all matter. Running them takes a platform, a subscription and the hours to learn it, and the jobs come first.

## Sound familiar?

Three moments when a small business may let work slip away.

**The electrician up a ladder.** The phone rings out twice before lunch. Both callers wanted someone this week, and by the time the ladder is down, they may have rung somebody else.

**The salon with a quiet profile.** Customers say the work is lovely at the till, but nobody asks them to say it on Google.

**The garage with last winter's customers.** Their names and numbers are in the system from last year's services, and nothing has gone to any of them since the invoice.

## How it works

1. **You keep your number.** We set up a hosted account and divert the calls you miss to it. The number on your van and your website does not change.
2. **Every missed call gets a text.** Within seconds, from a number tied to your business, saying who missed the call and how to reply or book. Replies land in one inbox and a phone app.
3. **Bookings go in the calendar.** Confirmations and reminders of each booking go out with nothing promotional in them.
4. **You see the counts.** Once a month: calls missed, texts sent, replies and bookings.

## Three plans, one account

- **Answer** is the phone: the text-back, the inbox, the calendar and the report.
- **Reputation** adds the review programme, with every customer asked, nobody incentivised and nothing hidden, and replies to reviews drafted for your approval. It also keeps your Google Business Profile up to date in your own account.
- **Follow-Up** adds two campaigns a month to the people you may lawfully message, one automation built or reviewed each month, a reactivation campaign each quarter on the records that can carry it, and a landing page a quarter.
- **The AI Receptionist** can be added to any plan, to answer the call rather than text it back.

Each part is also a service of its own — [missed-call text-back](/services/missed-call-text-back), [review management](/services/review-management), [Google Business Profile management](/services/google-business-profile), [email and SMS marketing](/services/email-sms-marketing), [landing pages](/services/landing-pages) and the [AI receptionist](/services/ai-receptionist). The plans bundle the running of them into one monthly fee and one inbox.

## What the starting price buys

Answer: the hosted account, with a UK number registered to your business or a divert from the one you publish; the text, written with you and tested from a real phone; one inbox and app for replies and website chat, with WhatsApp at cost for customers who opt in; the booking calendar with its reminders; and the monthly report.

**Move up:** Reputation or Follow-Up. **Add to any plan:** the AI Receptionist. Each is a change to the plan, not a new contract; set-up is charged per module added.

## What keeps you safe

Everything else we make lives in an account you own; these plans are hosted under our licence, so the terms are written down. You stay in charge of your customers' data (the controller, in law), and we process it for you under a written contract. The platform runs in the United States, so the data does not stay in the UK. If you leave, you get an export and the option to take the account with you, following a checklist written at set-up; a domain we assigned does not move with it. We do not host patient records. None of this is legal advice.

## What it costs

From £199 a month excl. VAT, plus a one-off set-up fee; all three plans are priced in the questions below. Usage is billed on top at cost from a published price list in pounds. Set-up is paid on signature, the plan monthly in advance and usage in arrears; there is no minimum term, and thirty days' notice ends it.

## When something else fits better

If the system is core to how you sell, or your team will live in it every day, own the account: that is [CRM automation](/services/crm-automation). If you need one part rather than a plan, each has its own page, linked above. If the website is the problem, a plan will not fix it: see [web development](/services/web-development). Paid ads and social posting are not in any plan.

Start with Answer. It is the cheapest plan to run and the easiest to leave, and its report shows what the phone is doing before you add anything. [Get in touch](/contact) with the number you publish and roughly how many calls you miss in a week, or read [how a project runs](/process) first.
