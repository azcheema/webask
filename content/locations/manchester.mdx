## Two Manchester facts that are easy to read as one

Greater Manchester is a metropolitan county of **2,867,800** residents (ONS Census 2021). Note
the basis: that is the whole county, not the Manchester City Council area, and the two are not
interchangeable.

Manchester the city is also the **second most competitive major UK city** for local search, on
SERPTool's 2026 ranking of 25 UK cities by local SEO opportunity, with financial services in
Spinningfields approaching London density.

Our own city research sets a population figure and the competitiveness ranking side by side, and
read that way the second looks like a consequence of the first. They are two claims about two
different things.
The competitiveness ranking describes **head terms** — _web design Manchester_, _digital agency
Manchester_ — which this page is built to hold, and does not expect to win first. What it says
very little about is the qualified searches that end in an enquiry: a different results page,
with a different set of incumbents on it. How different, in volume and in difficulty, is exactly
what has not been measured yet. The next section says why.

The easier page to write would set the two figures side by side and let the implication stand.
This is the version that will still be true when your first ranking report lands.

## The filter we will apply, and the number we admit we do not have

New targets will be held to a difficulty ceiling of **KD ≤ 35** — by default, and lower where
possible — and service head terms sit **last** in the priority order, worth pursuing once a
domain has authority behind it, never at launch. That rule bites us before it bites anyone else:
it is why this site is not chasing _seo agency uk_.

Then the caveat, which matters more than the rule, because the rule cannot yet be applied to
anything. The UK keyword map behind this site holds **332 terms**, and every one of them carries
`msv: null` and `kd: null`. No Ahrefs or Semrush pull has been run against it, so nothing has
ever been measured against that ceiling. Inventing plausible-looking volumes would fabricate
exactly the numbers that decide where months of work go, so the file records the gap rather than
papering over it. When difficulty is quoted against your terms it will come from a real pull with
a UK locale filter — and the spelling is part of the pull, because _optimisation_ and
_optimization_ are not the same keyword and do not carry the same volume.

## What "specific" looks like across Greater Manchester

Three axes. This hub is built on all three, rather than on repeating a city name in more places.

**By sector.** The county's mix runs professional and financial services, digital and technology,
healthcare and aesthetics, higher education, and media and creative. A page written for a
Spinningfields accountancy practice and a page written for a clinic have almost nothing in common
but the postcode. One page trying to serve both has to generalise until it is specific to
neither, and it splits the relevance each of them needs concentrated. That is why every term in
the keyword map is assigned exactly one target URL, and why a term listed twice fails the build.
Where the sector is regulated, the page is partly a compliance exercise before it is a marketing
one: [aesthetic clinics](/industries/aesthetic-clinics) is the worked example, and the
advertising rules there rule some search terms out before anyone weighs their volume.

**By district.** Deansgate, Spinningfields, the Northern Quarter, Ancoats, Didsbury, Chorlton. A
district page earns its URL only if it has something true and particular to say about who trades
there and what they need. Otherwise it is a find-and-replace, and that is precisely what the
uniqueness gate is set to reject. District pages here will ship `noindex, follow` and earn their
way into the index only by clearing it — none is written yet, and the same discipline would go
into yours.

**By what you actually do.** Service-plus-district and service-plus-sector phrases are longer and
less contested than the head term, and the person typing one is closer to buying. It is why our
own priority order puts service-by-area above service head terms — a structural judgement rather
than a measured one, for the reason given above. [SEO](/services/seo) is where that mapping gets
done; [web development](/services/web-development) is where the architecture to hold it gets
built, because a site organised around one loud phrase has nowhere to put fifty specific ones. If
you are weighing a rebuild against patching what you have,
[custom versus WordPress in 2026](/blog/custom-website-vs-wordpress-2026) runs the published UK
bands out over three years, and explains why they split by supplier rather than by platform.

## No Google Business Profile — and the worked example is this city

The cost should be stated before the justification.

WebAsk is fully remote. There is no UK office and no staffed UK address. Google requires a real
location with staff present during stated opening hours; virtual offices, mailbox services,
unstaffed coworking and PO boxes are policy violations that risk suspension and the permanent
loss of a listing. There is no eligible profile for us to create, and we are not going to invent
one against a borrowed address.

The bill is specific. **No Map Pack eligibility** — the three-result block above the blue links is
unreachable. **No Maps pin. No Google reviews**, which is the strongest local trust signal an SMB
has when it is choosing an agency, and a top local ranking factor. **No knowledge panel.** That is
a real cost and it should not be minimised. The example our own strategy uses to illustrate it is,
word for word, _web design agency Manchester_: rankable organically, never in the map.

What makes it survivable is who is doing the searching. Someone choosing an agency researches;
they are not tapping a pin the way someone searching for an emergency plumber is.

**It would not be survivable for you.** If you run a clinic, a shop or a same-day trade anywhere
in Greater Manchester, the profile is free, it sits above the blue links, and it should be
finished before you spend a pound on a retainer. One rule attached to it does not bend. Since
6 April 2025 the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 has made it illegal to write,
commission or publish fake reviews, or to publish an incentivised one without disclosing the
incentive — cash, a discount, a freebie, an event invitation or a financial interest — and the CMA
can determine a breach and fine directly — up to **10% of global annual turnover or £300,000,
whichever is higher**. Our own line sits inside that: never bought, never incentivised, disclosed
or not. The [2026 local SEO checklist](/blog/local-seo-checklist-2026) works through the rest of
it.

## Measuring it without the vanity rank

If the head term is not the target, a ranking screenshot is not the report.

The plan for this site is written down, in horizons counted from cutover rather than from a
signature: impressions appearing on service-and-city terms around months two to three — blue links
only, no map pack — the compliance long-tail starting to earn around months three to six, and
position movement on service-plus-city terms somewhere across months six to twelve.

The north star underneath all of it is qualified discovery calls booked per month, not average
position. Your scope would carry an equivalent timetable and an equivalent north star, agreed
before work starts rather than reverse-engineered afterwards from whatever happened. Both are
worth asking for in writing — from us, or from anyone else quoting you for Manchester search.
