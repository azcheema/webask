Say your HubSpot renewal is a few months off, and two of the seats you pay for sit empty. You are weighing GoHighLevel to put booking, texts and calls behind one login. Before migrating from HubSpot to GoHighLevel, you want to know what survives the move: your records, your phone number, and the record of who asked you to stop emailing them.

Checked against HubSpot's and HighLevel's published help pages on 2 October 2026. Both vendors change their products and pages, so check each linked page before you act.

## When not to migrate

In the words of our [CRM and automation](/services/crm-automation) page, moving from HubSpot to GoHighLevel is worth asking about when you are paying for seats nobody fills. Where a sales team works in HubSpot every day, migrating is disruption you pay for twice.

Empty seats may be a renewal question instead. HubSpot's [cancellation page](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account/how-do-i-cancel-my-hubspot-account) (updated 9 July 2026) says a downgrade at renewal can mean fewer seats or a lower tier, but "Mid-contract downgrades are not permitted". Leaving seats unused "will not automatically downgrade your account" ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account/review-your-upcoming-renewal), updated 9 September 2026).

Which platform fits is covered in our [GoHighLevel buyer's guide](/blog/what-is-gohighlevel-2026-buyers-guide).

### HighLevel's HubSpot Importer

HighLevel's [importer page](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007947-import-data-from-hubspot) (updated 10 September 2026) says you can "connect HubSpot, choose what to bring over, and start the import from inside HighLevel". So the HubSpot exports in the table are your own copy, and the input only if you import by CSV. Beyond the limits in the table, it says "Some associations may not be imported due to platform limits". On files, it says both "File attachments are supported for Contacts and Opportunities" and, under troubleshooting, "File imports aren't supported yet". Export your files from HubSpot either way.

Before you start, it says to "Use a clean account when possible", turn on "Allow duplicates" (it says you can re-enable de-duplication after the import), and "test imports in a new or non-production location". Afterwards, a report lists errors and warnings. The page does not mention opt-outs or consent, so treat those as their own job.

### If you import by CSV instead

Create custom fields and pipelines first. "Every Opportunity must be associated with a contact", and "Completed imports do not have a one-click undo option" ([HighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002517-importing-opportunities-using-a-csv-file), updated 17 September 2026). So tag each import, to find and delete it later. The [format page](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005143-csv-file-format-for-importing-contacts-and-opportunities) (updated 25 February 2026) recommends E.164 phone numbers, which start with the country code. The date formats it lists are all month-first or year-first, so convert UK-style dates. HighLevel's pages give different file-size limits, so check the current one.

## Carrying consent and opt-outs across

Who you may email, text or call is in our guide to [PECR for B2B outreach](/blog/pecr-b2b-outreach-rules-uk). This section is about keeping the record.

### What HubSpot holds on opt-outs and consent

- **Unsubscribes.** The "Unsubscribed from all email" and "Opted out of email" properties hold opt-outs ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/marketing-email/learn-a-contact-s-marketing-email-subscription-status), updated 22 September 2026).
- **The address, not the contact.** "Email subscription preferences are tied to an email address, rather than to a contact" ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/records/manage-your-subscription-preferences-and-types), updated 18 June 2026).
- **Opt-outs that are not contacts.** Addresses from an [opt-out import](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/marketing-email/import-an-opt-out-list) (updated 22 September 2026) are not "created as contacts". HubSpot says they appear "if you ever export bounced and unsubscribed contacts". We found no steps for that export, so ask HubSpot.
- **Legal basis.** The "Legal basis for processing contact's data" property holds HubSpot's own labels ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/contacts/how-can-i-track-lawful-basis-of-processing-in-hubspot), updated 28 September 2026). It may be blank if [data privacy settings](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/privacy-and-consent/how-do-i-turn-on-gdpr-functionality-in-my-hubspot-account) (updated 11 September 2026) were never turned on.
- **History.** A [property history export](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/properties/export-property-history) (updated 8 September 2026) can show who or what changed each value, for "up to 45 revisions" per contact property.
- **Forms.** If your forms asked for consent, export the submissions too.

HubSpot suggests you "may wish to delete" hard bounces and spam complaints ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/marketing-email/how-can-i-delete-contacts-who-have-unsubscribed-or-hard-bounce), updated 21 September 2026). Our build standard is to export first.

### Where opt-outs land in GoHighLevel

A DND column in a CSV applies DND "for all communication channels (SMS, Email, Calls, Voicemail, etc.)". For one channel, "you need to use a workflow and tags instead of the DND column" ([HighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000003905-importing-contacts-and-opportunities-via-csv), updated 16 September 2026). Build that workflow before the import. HighLevel's [DND page](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001214849-how-to-use-do-not-disturb-dnd-) (updated 28 September 2026) suggests fields such as "Consent Source, Opt-in Timestamp", and shows who changed each DND setting.

### Our build standard for the consent record

- The suppression record (every unsubscribe, opt-out-only address and hard bounce) goes in after the main import and before anything sends: a CSV import set to "Create and Update", with channel tags for the per-channel DND workflow rather than a DND column. HighLevel checks for existing records by contact ID, then email, then phone ([HighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000004432-importing-contacts-using-a-csv-file), updated 18 March 2026), so it updates the imported contacts.
- Each imported contact carries its source, its basis and the date that basis was recorded, or it waits in a review queue.
- While both systems run, only one sends marketing, and an opt-out in either is applied in both on the same day.

## Email sending from the new platform

By default, emails show HighLevel's sending servers in the "sent via" headers ([HighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001226115-dedicated-email-sending-domains-overview-setup), updated 17 April 2026). HighLevel says dedicated sending domains "apply only to accounts on the LC Email system", so check which email system your account uses first. HighLevel recommends "a unique subdomain not used for any other purpose". If the domain you connect already points to another email service, HighLevel says to "remove any existing MX or SPF records" from it first. Our build standard is a fresh subdomain, so nothing set up for HubSpot's sending has to be removed while both run.

Warm-up starts on its own for a new domain, with a daily limit of 1,000 emails in stage one, and HighLevel says to "Respect daily warmup limits to avoid future enforcement blocks" ([HighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005242-domain-warmup-how-it-works-fixed-stage-model-), updated 30 June 2026).

## Moving UK phone numbers

First, find where each number lives. Porting a number into HubSpot "makes HubSpot the owner of that phone number" ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/calling/port-out-a-hubspot-phone-number), updated 8 September 2026). A number still with your own provider only needs the GoHighLevel steps. HighLevel's [moving-numbers guide](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006369-moving-phone-numbers-migration-guide) (updated 4 September 2026) says whether a number is already hosted by Twilio or by another carrier "determines whether the numbers are migrated or ported", so tell HighLevel Support which carrier holds each number.

**Out of HubSpot.** For "a HubSpot number that you own in your account", you ask the new provider for a port-in, and it should send the request to "HubSpot’s call carrier, Twilio". It "can take up to five business days" for the carrier to share the PIN and account details. After the port, it "won’t be available for use in your HubSpot account".

**Into GoHighLevel.** HighLevel's standard flow "currently supports porting in numbers from the United States only" ([HighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001211919-porting-options-us-in-app-vs-international-manual-process), updated 3 September 2026). For a UK number, you contact HighLevel Support. For a number from another carrier, Support "can submit Twilio's International Porting Form on your behalf". A HubSpot number sits with Twilio, so ask Support which route it takes. Your name and address "must match your carrier records exactly". "Carriers do not guarantee exact dates/times."

**The UK rule on both platforms.** Every UK long code (local, national, mobile and toll-free numbers, in HighLevel's words) needs an approved UK Regulatory Compliance (RC) bundle to send messages or make calls ([HubSpot](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/calling/calling-supported-countries-and-phone-number-availability), updated 14 September 2026; [HighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002831-know-your-customer-kyc-in-the-united-kingdom), updated 18 July 2024). HighLevel says a business not registered in the UK "should use UK Mobile or UK Toll Free numbers".

**Keep the old service live.** "Do not delete, release, disable, disconnect, or cancel a phone number with its current provider before a migration or port is confirmed complete" ([HighLevel](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006369-moving-phone-numbers-migration-guide), updated 4 September 2026). Text history stays behind: porting "does not migrate historic logs/conversations". Export it first.

## UK GDPR when the new platform is hosted in the US

This section summarises the published guidance of the Information Commission (the ICO) and the vendors' documents. WebAsk is not a law firm, and this is not legal advice.

**You stay the controller.** HighLevel's [data processing addendum](https://www.gohighlevel.com/data-processing-agreement) (last updated July 2026) says "HighLevel will act as a Processor" and the customer "will act as the Controller". The ICO says that "every time a controller uses a processor", there must be a written contract ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/when-is-a-contract-needed-and-why-is-it-important/), under review). It must say the processor acts only on your "documented instructions (including when making an international transfer of personal data) unless it is required to do otherwise by UK law" ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/what-needs-to-be-included-in-the-contract/), under review).

**The data goes to the US.** "Our product infrastructure resides in the United States", says HighLevel's [security overview](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000000574-highlevel-security-and-compliance-overview) (updated 16 June 2026). Its addendum says HighLevel "is certified to" the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF). The ICO says you must only rely on that if the US business "has an active status on the DPF list", has self-certified to the UK Extension, and covers the type of data you send ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/adequacy-regulations/how-does-the-uk-extension-to-the-eu-us-data-privacy-framework-work/)). So check HighLevel's entry on the [DPF list](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list) yourself. The ICO says you "should undertake periodic checks".

If you cannot rely on the UK Extension, the government's [UK-US data bridge factsheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-us-data-bridge-supporting-documents/uk-us-data-bridge-factsheet-for-uk-organisations) (published 21 September 2023) points to safeguards such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum. It also says UK organisations "should be mindful of the need to update privacy policies".

**Special category data.** The ICO says "You must take additional steps for certain types of special category data and criminal offence data", and its [UK Extension page](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/international-transfers/adequacy-regulations/how-does-the-uk-extension-to-the-eu-us-data-privacy-framework-work/) lists which types.

HighLevel's own overview sets the limit: "use of the HighLevel product alone does not make you GDPR compliant".

## A step-by-step migration checklist

1. Write down why you are moving. If it is seats, ask HubSpot about a downgrade first.
2. Find your renewal date and [download your Order Form](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account/manage-your-hubspot-subscription) (updated 28 September 2026). Once you decide, turn off auto-renewal.
3. List the workflows, sequences, forms, pages and reports you use, with an image of each workflow.
4. Export unsubscribes and hard bounces, and ask HubSpot how to export opt-out-only addresses.
5. Export legal-basis values, property history with source, and form submissions.
6. Export all records, a backup if your tier has one, files, email HTML and page HTML.
7. Open GoHighLevel in your name, read the addendum, check the DPF list, update your privacy notice.
8. Create the consent fields. For a CSV import, also create custom fields and pipelines first.
9. Import into a test location first, read the error report, then import into the live one.
10. Import the suppression record as a Create and Update CSV, tagged by channel for the DND workflow, before anything sends.
11. On LC Email, set up a sending subdomain and let warm-up start.
12. Rebuild and test each workflow, form and template before switching it on.
13. Export text history first. Submit the RC bundle and ask HighLevel Support to move each UK number. Keep the old line live.
14. Run both systems inside the HubSpot term, with one sending marketing.
15. Replace every embedded HubSpot form on your website.
16. Take the final exports before the term ends, and keep them.
