Most med spas don't have a demand problem. People want these treatments. They have a "the clinic across town shows up first, books easier, and follows up better" problem. Marketing that works here is a system, not a burst of ads.

<Callout variant="note" title="TL;DR">
  The med spas that grow in 2026 win on three things: getting found locally, turning visitors into
  bookings, and keeping clients coming back. Ads can amplify a funnel that works — they can't fix
  one that leaks. Fix the funnel first.
</Callout>

Here is the playbook we would run, in order.

## The three stages that matter

Every med spa's growth comes down to the same funnel. Get one stage wrong and the rest leaks.

## Stage 1: Get found

When someone searches _aesthetic clinic near me_ or _skin clinic in your city_, you want to be in the map pack and on the first page. That comes down to local SEO: a complete Google Business Profile, a real page for each area you serve, and a steady stream of recent reviews.

Reviews do double duty here — they rank you and they close the sale. In aesthetics, trust is everything, and recent, specific reviews are the strongest trust signal you have.

## Stage 2: Convert

Getting found is wasted if the visit doesn't turn into a booking. Three things move the needle:

- **A fast, mobile-first site.** Most of your traffic is on a phone. A slow site loses the booking before the page loads.
- **One-tap booking.** Every extra step costs you appointments. Let people book in seconds, day or night.
- **Clear pricing.** "Starting at" pricing builds trust and filters for serious clients. Hiding it sends people to a competitor who shows it.

<Callout variant="warning">
  Aesthetic and medical treatments are regulated. Don't promise specific results, use
  before-and-after photos without written consent, or make medical claims you can't back up. Honest,
  compliant marketing isn't just safer — in this field it's what earns trust.
</Callout>

## Stage 3: Retain

The cheapest booking is the one from a client you already have. Most med spas pour money into new leads while their best growth is sitting in their client list.

- Follow up after every visit — a simple, automated message.
- Prompt the rebooking before the client leaves, or shortly after.
- Offer memberships or packages that reward coming back.

This is where CRM and automation earn their cost: they run the follow-up and rebooking for you, so nothing depends on someone remembering.

Ads have their place — but as an amplifier, not a patch. Turn them on once the funnel converts.

## Find your biggest leak first

Before you spend a dollar on marketing, measure three numbers:

1. **Where you rank** for your top treatment plus your city.
2. **Your booking rate** — visitors to your site who actually book.
3. **Your rebooking rate** — clients who come back within a set window.

Whichever is weakest is your biggest leak. Fix that first. Spending on ads while your booking rate is low just pays to fill a bucket with a hole in it.
