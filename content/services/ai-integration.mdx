## AI that earns its keep, not a science project

There are two kinds of AI projects. The first is the demo that impresses in a meeting and never touches the business again. The second is the unglamorous one that quietly answers the phone at 8pm, books the appointment, and saves an afternoon of copying data between two systems. We build the second kind.

The difference is not the model — everyone has access to the same frontier models. The difference is integration and discipline: woven into the tools you already use, scoped to a job worth doing, and measured so you know it is actually working. AI that lives in a separate tab nobody opens is a cost; AI wired into your funnel is leverage.

## What Naxdor AI integration does differently

Three things separate our work from a flashy proof-of-concept:

1. **It is woven into your existing tools.** AI that talks to your CRM, your calendar, and your data sources — not a standalone toy you have to babysit and copy-paste around.
2. **It is scoped honestly.** We start by mapping what AI should and, just as importantly, should not do for you. Pointing it at the wrong job is how these projects waste money.
3. **It is measured.** We build an evaluation harness so quality is something we test against, not something we assume. When the AI handles a customer, you can see how well — and tune it.

That is the floor. We deliver three kinds of integration, often in combination.

## AI Voice Agents

A voice agent answers your phone when you cannot — after hours, during a rush, or on the fifth simultaneous call that would otherwise go to voicemail. It can greet callers, answer common questions, qualify the lead, book an appointment straight into your calendar, and hand off to a human when the conversation needs one. For a business that lives and dies by inbound calls — clinics, home services, practices — a missed call is a missed customer, and a voice agent is the difference between capturing that lead and losing it to whoever picked up.

We build the agent around your actual call flows, connect it to your booking and CRM, and tune it on real conversations so it sounds like your business, not a robot reading a script.

## AI Chatbots & Assistants

A chatbot on your site or in your messaging channels answers questions, captures leads, and books appointments around the clock — but only if it answers from _your_ information, not a generic guess. We build retrieval-grounded assistants: the bot answers from your documents, your services, and your policies, so it is accurate and on-brand instead of confidently wrong. It can qualify visitors, route them to the right place, and pass a warm lead into your CRM with the context already captured, so your team picks up a conversation that is already half-won.

## AI Workflow Automation

The least visible and often most valuable category: the repetitive admin that eats your team's hours. Pulling data between tools, generating documents, triaging inbound messages, updating records in three systems when one thing changes. We build these as reliable workflows — using automation platforms like n8n or Make, with AI in the steps that genuinely need judgment — so the busywork runs itself and your people do the work only they can do. The test is simple: if a task is repetitive, rule-based, and someone does it by hand every week, it is a candidate.
