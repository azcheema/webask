## Landing page design for the campaign your website cannot host

There is an offer to run, an event to fill or an advert to switch on, and the page it needs does not exist. The website was built by someone who no longer answers, or adding a page means a ticket and a wait, and the campaign is next week.

## Sound familiar?

**The seasonal offer.** Boiler services before winter, a spring treatment, a Black Friday price. It needs its own page with one button, and the home page has a button for everything else.

**The open day.** A date, a map, a form, and a follow-up message to the people who book. Nothing on the site does any of that.

**The Google Ads campaign with nowhere to land.** The advert is written and the budget is set; the click goes to a home page that talks about everything except the thing in the advert.

## How it works

1. **One page, one job.** No navigation to wander off into. A headline that matches the advert or the offer, the reasons, the form or the booking calendar, and one ask.
2. **Built the right way for the job.** If visitors come from an advert, an email or a QR code, and getting the page live quickly matters most, we build it in the platform's page builder, inside your account. If it has to rank in search or live inside a site whose standards you paid for, we build it in code, alongside a website we build for you. The first call decides, and the reason goes into the scope.
3. **Wired to your CRM.** Every lead from the form or the calendar goes straight into your CRM, with consent captured at entry in words the person can understand. The follow-up message says who sent it and how to stop. Nothing is retyped.
4. **Measured.** You can see which button was pressed and which campaign sent the visit. Thirty days after launch you get a report: visits, enquiries, bookings, where they came from, and what we would change.

## What keeps you safe

Measuring a campaign page sets cookies; anything non-essential waits for a clear yes, with accept and reject weighted equally and nothing pre-ticked. Since 5 February 2026, storage whose sole purpose is statistical, such as counting visits to improve the page, may run without consent if the visitor is told its purpose and given a simple way to object. Advertising and cross-site tracking still wait for a yes. A deposit or booking from a consumer not in front of you can be a distance contract; the customer must then be told, before they are bound, whether they can cancel and how. That wording and the terms link go on the page, not behind it. We check the builder's defaults before launch.
