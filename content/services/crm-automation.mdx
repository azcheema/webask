## Leads come in and fall through the cracks? A CRM set up to catch them

An enquiry arrives through the website form on a Saturday evening, another by text, a third as a missed call. Each lands somewhere different, nobody owns the follow-up, and by Wednesday the one that mattered may have booked elsewhere. The system is built on GoHighLevel first and HubSpot where it fits, wired to UK consent rules from the first form.

## Sound familiar?

Three situations where following up can quietly become nobody's job.

**The clinic inbox on a Monday.** Nobody can say which weekend enquiries were answered, or which consultation requests are still waiting from Friday.

**The quote nobody chased.** A kitchen fitter sends a quote on Tuesday and means to follow it up on Friday. Friday is a full day on site. The quote sits in a sent folder, and the customer may go with the firm that rang back.

**The HubSpot bill.** A consultancy pays per seat for a marketing hub it uses a fraction of, while its contacts sit across that, a spreadsheet and an old email tool. Moving to GoHighLevel has come up, but nobody knows what would survive the move, or which of those contacts said no to email two tools ago.

## How it works

1. **A first call about your contacts, not the feature list.** We ask where leads come from and what you run today. Then one question about the list you already hold: for each contact, can you say where the record came from and on what basis you may email it? The answer shapes the platform and the scope, and you get a straight answer on whether moving is worth it, including when it is not.
2. **A written scope with a date in it.** You get the scope and the go-live date in writing, not an estimate on a call.
3. **Built in an account you own.** The subscription is in your name, and each channel in the scope is tested end to end.
4. **Consent built into the fields, not a policy document.** Each contact captured through the new forms carries its legal form, set at capture rather than guessed later, and a record of where it came from. Automations check both before adding anyone, and a contact with no basis on which you may email it waits for a person to review it instead of being sent anything.
5. **Launch, training and thirty days of tuning.** Whoever will run the system is trained on it, and the first thirty days after launch are for adjusting it against real enquiries.

## What the starting price buys

A working system for one business, not just a logged-in account. Enquiries are captured with consent recorded, followed up automatically and reported by where they came from, with a booking calendar in the same system.

**Add when you need it:** a migration from HubSpot or another CRM · more pipelines and sequences · further booking and messaging integrations · white-label GoHighLevel with a sub-account per client and a snapshot to clone from. ## GoHighLevel or HubSpot, and moving between them

The platform should follow your business, not our preference.

**GoHighLevel** fits service businesses that want pipeline, booking, messaging and call handling behind one login, instead of five subscriptions held together by integrations that break quietly. It also fits agencies that want to resell it under their own brand.

**HubSpot** fits teams that want a polished, widely integrated platform with room to grow into fuller marketing and sales hubs. HubSpot implementation and onboarding set up its forms, workflows and reporting around how work reaches you, with the same consent fields as GoHighLevel.

**Moving from HubSpot to GoHighLevel** is worth asking about when you are paying for seats nobody fills. Where a sales team works in HubSpot every day, migrating is disruption you pay for twice, and you will hear that on the first call rather than after the invoice. GoHighLevel's own plans and tiers are compared in [our GoHighLevel buyer's guide](/blog/what-is-gohighlevel-2026-buyers-guide).

## What keeps you safe

**The account is yours**, in your name, so access to your customers never depends on us.

**Sequences check before they send.** Marketing email or text to a limited company, LLP or public body needs no prior consent under PECR, but must name the sender and how to stop. A sole trader, unincorporated partnership or personal address needs consent or the soft opt-in: details from a sale or negotiations for one, your own similar products and services only, and a simple means of refusing at collection and in every message since.

**Migrations carry the consent.** A CSV export carries the address, not the lawful basis, opt-out history or source. Records the old system cannot vouch for arrive unconsented.

**No bought lists.** We do not buy, rent or supply lists, and we will not import one you were sold.

For [aesthetic clinics](/industries/aesthetic-clinics) and [dental practices](/industries/dental-practices), advertising and recall rules shape sequences too.
