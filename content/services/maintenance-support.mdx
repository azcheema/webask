## Software decays when nobody is looking after it

A website, app, or CRM is not a painting you hang on the wall and admire for years. It is software, and software rots when it is left alone — not in a dramatic way, but quietly. Dependencies fall behind and open security holes. A browser or OS update breaks something that worked yesterday. Performance drifts as content piles up. A form silently stops delivering leads and nobody notices for a month.

The owner who skips maintenance to save a few hundred dollars a month usually pays for it later — in a hacked site, a costly emergency fix, or six months of leads lost to a broken form. A care plan is the unglamorous insurance that keeps the asset you invested in actually working.

## What a Naxdor care plan covers

Every plan is built on the same foundation:

1. **Security patching and dependency upgrades** on a regular cadence — so vulnerabilities are closed before they are exploited, not after.
2. **Uptime, performance, and error monitoring with alerts to us** — so when something breaks, we usually see it and fix it before you (or a customer) notice.
3. **A monthly bucket of improvement hours** — copy edits, small features, fixes, and tweaks, so the asset keeps improving instead of just standing still.
4. **A quarterly health review** with clear recommendations, and **priority response** on anything that does break.

The point is simple: the things we build are long-term assets, and a care plan is how they stay that way.

## Who care plans are for

Care plans are designed first for clients whose site, app, or CRM we built — keeping it current without you needing an in-house engineer. We also take on **inherited stacks**: software someone else built that you now need maintained. For an inherited property we start with a short audit to understand what we are taking responsibility for, then bring it onto a plan. We are honest if something is in poor enough shape that maintaining it is throwing good money after bad — sometimes the right call is a focused fix or a rebuild, not a care plan over a cracked foundation.

## What we don't build

Honesty about scope is half the value of a service page. For Naxdor care plans, the most common asks that sit outside the monthly fee:

- **Major new features or rebuilds.** Significant new work is quoted separately as a project — the monthly improvement hours are for tweaks and small features, not a second version.
- **Paid platform license fees.** Hosting, CRM seats, and similar subscriptions are yours and passed through at cost; the care plan is our time, not your vendors' bills.
