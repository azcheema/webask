## Email and SMS marketing for the past customers nobody messages

They are all in the system: the people who booked last year, the ones who asked for a quote and went quiet, the customers who have not heard from you since their last booking. The CRM is live and silent. If writing to them never reaches the top of the list, or nobody is sure who may be written to, this plan is for you.

## Sound familiar?

**A dental practice's lapsed patients.** Patients overdue a check-up, a recall that has reminded them once, and nothing set to follow it up.

**A salon's quiet month.** A month the diary expects to be light, and a list of regulars nobody has written to.

**A trade's annual reminders.** Last year's service dates are in the system, and nothing is set to remind anyone this year.

## How it works

1. **Month one is the audit, not a send.** Every record on your list is tagged by who it belongs to — a company, or a person — and by whether you may message them. Records you may not message are parked, not deleted, and not messaged to ask.
2. **Two email or text campaigns a month, written for you.** You send a few lines on what the month is about. We write each campaign in your voice and check it against the rules for your sector. Then we send it from your platform to the people who may receive it, and report on it two weeks later.
3. **One automation a month, built or reviewed.** A welcome after an enquiry, a follow-up after a quote, a rebooking prompt at the interval your service runs on, a win-back when a customer goes quiet. Each checks who may receive it before it sends.
4. **A list kept clean.** Bounces removed, opt-outs honoured in every tool, and every message naming who sent it and how to stop.
5. **A report that counts what you can check.** Replies, bookings and enquiries that followed a campaign, opt-outs, and the size of the list you may still message — not open rates, which rest on tracking pixels that many inboxes now block or pre-fetch.

## What the starting price buys

The consent audit, two campaigns a month, one automation built or reviewed, list hygiene, the monthly report, and up to 10,000 emails and 200 SMS segments (a segment is up to 160 characters in the basic character set) a month. There is no set-up fee, because the first month's audit is the set-up.

**Add when you need it:** four campaigns a month and a landing page each quarter · a WhatsApp channel for the customers who opted in · more SMS segments. Segments beyond the allowance, and every WhatsApp message, are passed through at cost.

## What keeps you safe

Who you may message depends on who they are, not how the address looks, so the audit tags every record before any send. Each automation we build or review reads the tag before it sends. For a clinic, a message promoting a treatment is an advert; for a prescription-only medicine, the compliant message promotes the consultation. A monthly fee, shown on the [pricing page](/pricing), and no set-up fee. What moves the price is campaigns a month, automations, the state of the consent records you hold, and text volume.
