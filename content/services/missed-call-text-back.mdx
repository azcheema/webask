A caller who reaches no one may not leave a voicemail. They can ring the next business on the list instead, and a call back at five may come after they have booked elsewhere.

## Sound familiar?

Three moments when the phone goes unanswered.

**The plumber under a boiler.** The phone rings while both hands are busy. A new customer who hears nothing back may not ring again.

**The salon on a Saturday.** The phone sits by the till and rings while every chair is busy. Someone who wanted a colour next week can just as easily ring the salon across the road.

**The garage at half past five.** The last call of the day could be someone whose car has just failed its MOT. They may want it booked before the weekend. The phone rings out, and the job may go with it.

## How a missed-call text-back service works

1. **You keep your number.** Unanswered calls are diverted to a line we set up. The number on your van, your website and your Google profile does not change, and switching the divert off puts everything back.
2. **The caller gets a text (an SMS) in seconds.** It says who missed the call, how to reply and how to book, and it comes from a number tied to your business. We write it with you and test it from a real phone before it goes live.
3. **Replies land in one inbox.** On a phone app and on a screen, so whoever is free picks it up, and the next person to look can see what was said.
4. **You see the counts.** Once a month: calls missed, texts sent, conversations that replied, bookings that followed a reply. Four counts taken straight from the system that sends the texts, with no estimates.

## What the starting price buys

The divert, the message written with you, the inbox and app, the test from a real phone and the monthly report. Usage is not included: whatever the platform charges for, such as the texts and replies, the calls on the divert and the number, is billed on top at cost, based on what you use.

**Add when you need it:** a new UK number instead of a divert · web chat and WhatsApp in the same inbox. Each is priced separately and can be added later; none is required to start.

## The text, word for word

> Hi, it's \{Business\}. Sorry we missed your call. Reply here and we'll get back to you, or book at \{short link\}. Reply STOP to opt out.

It names who sent it and says why. It gives the caller a way to reply, a way to book and a way to stop. At 147 characters, with a 24-character business name and a twelve-character link placeholder, it fits inside one 160-character text, so it arrives as one message and costs one message. The wording is yours to adjust within those five points.

## What keeps you safe

The text is a customer-service message, and it stays one only because of what it says. Add an offer or a discount and it becomes marketing sent to a number you hold no consent for, so it is a change we will not make. A review link stays out too, by design. The caller's number is used to reply to that call and for nothing else unless they ask, and if they reply STOP, the number is marked so that nothing further is sent.
