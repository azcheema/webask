## The calls you cannot answer are the jobs you do not get

Nobody is free, the phone rings out, and the caller does not leave a voicemail. Hiring someone to answer costs more than the calls are worth; answering them yourself means stopping the work you are paid for. So the phone becomes a thing you feel guilty about, and the enquiries go to whoever picked up.

## Sound familiar?

**The garage after closing.** The last MOT failure of the day rings at ten past six. The office is dark. The caller wants it booked before the weekend and rings the next garage instead.

**The salon at eleven on a Saturday.** Every chair is busy and the phone is by the till. Whoever answers loses a client mid-cut; whoever does not loses the booking.

**The sole trader on a ladder.** The number on the van is a mobile in a pocket. It rings four times a day at the worst moments, and the callbacks at five go to voicemail.

## How it works

1. **Your number stays.** The assistant sits behind a divert: out of hours only, on overflow when the line is engaged, or on every call. Changing which is a setting, not a project.
2. **It answers in your name and says what it is.** The first sentence names your business, says it is an automated assistant, and offers to book, take a message or put the caller through to a person.
3. **It does one of five things.** Books an appointment into the calendar you already run, takes a message, answers a question from the short list you approved, transfers to a person, or — when it cannot do any of those — takes the caller's details for you to ring back. If it fails, calls fall back to voicemail or a person automatically.
4. **You get the summary.** Who called, what they wanted, what happened — into your inbox and onto the contact's record. A call at nine at night is a booking or a message at nine the next morning, with nothing to retype.

## What keeps you safe

The assistant answers calls; it never makes them. The UK rules on automated telephony are written around calls a business makes, and an outbound call from a synthetic voice to market anything is where they bite hard, so the plan has no callback feature and will not gain one. It says it is automated in its first sentence, because a caller who believes they spoke to a person has been misled. Call recording is off unless you switch it on; when it is on, the same sentence says so, because callers should be told up front. Questions about treatments, legal or clinical matters are handed to a person every time, by design. [What UK rules say about AI on the phone →](/blog/ai-receptionist-uk-rules-costs)
