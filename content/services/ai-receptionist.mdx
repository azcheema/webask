## The calls you cannot answer, answered by an AI receptionist

You cannot answer every call, and hiring a receptionist may be more than you can justify. Answering the calls yourself means stopping the work you are paid for, and letting them ring out means a caller who wanted to book may try someone else.

## Sound familiar?

**The call that comes in during a call.** You are already on the phone with one customer when a second rings. It goes unanswered while you finish the first, and that caller may not wait.

**The lunchtime hour.** The front desk closes from one to two. The phone keeps ringing, and nobody is there to pick it up until two.

**The Sunday enquiry.** The business is closed, but the caller has a free moment and wants the booking made now, while they think of it.

## How the AI call answering service works

1. **Your number stays.** The assistant sits behind a divert: out of hours only, on overflow when the line is engaged, or on every call, day and night. Switching between them is a setting, not a project.
2. **It answers in your name and says what it is.** Its first sentence names your business and says it is an automated assistant; the exact words are below.
3. **It does one of five things.** It books an appointment into the calendar you already run, takes a message, answers a question from the short list you approved, or puts the caller through to a person. When it can do none of those, it takes their details so you can ring back. If it fails, calls fall back to voicemail or a person automatically.
4. **You get the summary.** Who called, what they wanted, what happened — into your inbox. A call answered after closing becomes a booking in your calendar or a message in your inbox, with nothing to retype.

## What the starting price buys

## The opening line, word for word

> Hello, you've reached \{Business\}. I'm an automated assistant. I can book an appointment or take a message, and I can put you through to a person. How can I help?

You hear it yourself on the test number before the plan goes live.

## What keeps you safe

The assistant answers calls; it never makes them, and will not gain a feature that does, because how an AI voice would be treated if it made marketing calls is unsettled. A caller can leave details for you to ring back. It says it is automated in its first sentence, because a caller who believes they spoke to a person has been misled. Call recording is off unless you switch it on; when it is on, the same sentence says so, because callers should be told up front. The assistant is scripted not to answer questions about treatments, legal or clinical matters; it takes the caller's details or transfers the call.
