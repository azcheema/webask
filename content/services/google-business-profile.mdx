## Half-filled, unloved, and below a competitor on the map

A profile can be claimed in an afternoon and never opened again. If that is your profile, the hours may have drifted out of date, the photographs stopped changing and customer questions gone unanswered, while another business nearby shows up above you on the map.

## Sound familiar?

**The unclaimed profile.** The profile exists, but nobody in the business claimed it or has the login. The category is wrong and the phone number is the old one.

**The second branch.** The new location's profile has barely any photos and no services listed, and reviews meant for it land on the other branch's profile.

**The suspended profile.** The profile has gone from the map with an email nobody understood, and the appeal has not been filed.

## How it works

1. **We check you qualify, first.** Google's test is short: a premises customers can visit, or a business that travels to them. We tell you on the first call whether your address passes, before anything is created.
2. **Your account, our access.** You hold the owner role; we are added as a manager, with your notice and consent. Google's guidelines ask that of a third party for menu links, and we apply it to everything we do on the profile. You can remove us in a minute without losing anything.
3. **We optimise the profile, once, in full.** We choose categories against what people search, list and describe every service, and fill in the attributes, opening and holiday hours, photographs and a description written for a customer. Your name, address and phone will match your website character for character.
4. **Then every week.** A post about something real and new photographs from a short brief to you. Questions from customers get an answer, and a suspicious competitor listing goes through Google's reporting process.
5. **You see what Google sees.** Once a month, from Google's own insights: how people found the profile, what they searched, how many called, asked for directions or clicked through.

## What the starting price buys

The eligibility check, then the profile set up properly once in your own account. After that, it is looked after every week, your listings on four other directories are kept in step by hand, and you get a monthly report. Per location, because the work is per profile.

**Add when you need it:** a suspension worked through and appealed · listings synced by tool across a long directory list · extra locations.

## What keeps you safe

A profile for an address nobody works from — a virtual office, a mailbox, your accountant's address — breaks Google's rules and can be suspended, and a suspension runs on Google's timetable, not ours. So we never create one, never verify a location we have not confirmed you operate from, and never hold the owner role on a profile that belongs to you.

For a clinic, the rule against advertising prescription-only medicines to the public applies anywhere the public can see it, the profile included, so its services and posts advertise the consultation, never the medicine.
