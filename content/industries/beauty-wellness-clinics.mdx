## The problem we solve for beauty and wellness clinics

Spas, IV bars, beauty studios, and wellness clinics live and die by the calendar. Revenue isn't a single big sale — it's a steady stream of bookings, memberships, and repeat visits. So the website's job isn't to look luxurious in a portfolio; it's to fill the schedule today and keep clients coming back next month. Most clinic sites are built for the first goal and quietly fail the second.

Here's where the revenue leaks:

- **Booking is harder than it should be.** A visitor ready to book has to call during business hours, or dig through a menu to find a scheduling link, or fill a form that goes to an inbox. Every bit of friction loses a booking to a competitor whose site lets them book in two taps.
- **First-time visitors don't come back.** You earn a first visit and then nothing happens — no follow-up, no nudge toward the next appointment, no path into a membership or package. The one-time visit was the whole relationship, when it should have been the start of one.
- **The recurring-revenue engine isn't built.** Memberships, prepaid packages, and gift cards are what make a wellness business stable, but they're treated as an afterthought instead of the core of the site and the automation behind it.

We build the booking-first site and the retention automation that turns first visits into regulars and one-off clients into members.

## Built around how clinics actually make money

Wellness businesses run on two engines: new bookings and retention. We build for both, and we treat retention as the one most clinics neglect.

The booking engine is about removing friction — booking, packages, and memberships available online, fast, on a phone, without a phone call. The retention engine is about automation done well: a follow-up after a first visit, a nudge to rebook before a client drifts, a loyalty reward, a win-back for the lapsed, and a review request at the right moment. For a spa or IV bar, the difference between a one-time visitor and a member is usually a few well-timed automated touches, not more manual work from a team that's already busy delivering treatments.

Your brand is visual and your traffic is mobile, so we don't trade speed for polish — we engineer both. A gorgeous site that loads slowly loses bookings to whoever opens first.

## How an engagement works

We start with where bookings and repeat visits come from, not with a mood board. A typical engagement runs Discover → Design → Build → Grow:

1. **Discover.** We map your booking and revenue model — single visits versus memberships and packages, where new clients come from, and where retention breaks down.
2. **Design.** We design the site and the booking, membership, and package flows together, so the revenue path is decided before it's built.
3. **Build.** We build the site fast and correct, wire in booking, recurring billing, and retention automation, integrate your payment provider, and set up call and message capture — tested before launch.
4. **Grow.** Local SEO and ongoing improvement compound the result over the following months. Every account stays in your name — site, domain, CRM, payment, and client data.
