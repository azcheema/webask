## The problem we solve for aesthetic clinics

Most med-spa and aesthetic-clinic websites are beautiful and book almost nothing. They were built to look like the brand — soft photography, an elegant logo, a gallery — by someone who never asked the only question that matters: when a prospect lands here from their phone at 9pm, what makes them book a consultation tonight instead of scrolling to the next clinic?

In aesthetics, the gap between traffic and bookings is where the money leaks out, and it leaks in a few predictable places:

- **The site is slow and the booking path is buried.** Your traffic is overwhelmingly mobile and your pages are image-heavy. A gallery that takes five seconds to load on a phone loses the visitor before they ever find the "Book" button — which is often three taps deep behind a menu anyway.
- **The phone goes to voicemail.** You're with a client, mid-treatment, hands occupied. The prospect who calls hits voicemail, hangs up, and books with whoever answers. That's not a staffing failure; it's a structural one, and it's the single biggest leak we see in this vertical.
- **Leads die in an inbox.** A consultation request comes in, someone means to follow up, the day gets busy, and three days later the lead has cooled. There's no system catching it, reminding it, and nudging it toward a booked, deposit-secured appointment.

We build the conversion path the pretty site forgot. The look still matters — credibility in aesthetics is visual — but it sits on top of an engine designed to turn a late-night phone search into a confirmed appointment.

## Built around how clinics actually win patients

A few things are specific to aesthetics, and we build for them rather than against them.

Patient searches are intent-rich and local — "lip filler near me," "morpheus8 cost," "best med spa in [city]." Treatment pages are built to match that intent and the structure search engines reward, so the people already looking for what you offer can find you. Galleries are engineered to stay fast and credible on a small screen, because in this industry the before-and-after _is_ the sales pitch.

We're also candid about the compliance edges, because they're real here. Consent for patient images, the advertising rules around before-and-afters, and how injectable brand names can be used in marketing vary by state and by platform. We're a web and software firm, not your regulatory counsel — but we'll build so you can show results credibly and flag where you should check a rule before you publish, rather than leaving you to find out the hard way.

## How an engagement works

We don't start by talking about pixels. We start with where your bookings come from and where they're being lost. A typical engagement runs Discover → Design → Build → Grow:

1. **Discover.** We map your real booking journey — how prospects find you, where they drop off, what your front desk is drowning in, and which treatments drive the revenue you want more of.
