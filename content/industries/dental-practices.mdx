## The problem we solve for dental practices

Your next new patient is on a phone right now, searching "dentist near me" or "emergency dentist open today." Whether they land on you or the practice down the road comes down to three things most dental sites get wrong: whether you show up in that search at all, whether the site loads fast and makes booking effortless, and whether anyone follows up when they reach out.

The pattern repeats across practices we talk to:

- **You're invisible in local search.** A template site with thin content and no local-SEO foundation doesn't rank for the terms that bring new patients. The practice that shows up first wins the call, and right now that isn't you.
- **The front desk is overwhelmed and calls get missed.** Between checkouts, insurance questions, and a ringing phone, calls drop. A missed call from someone in pain is a patient who dials the next practice on the list.
- **No-shows and lapsed patients bleed revenue quietly.** Empty chairs from forgotten appointments and patients overdue for a cleaning are recurring losses — and they're almost entirely preventable with automation that most practices simply don't have running.

We build the system that closes those gaps: a site that ranks and books, a way to catch every call, and automation that keeps the schedule full and patients coming back.

## Built around how practices actually grow

Dentistry has its own rhythms, and we build for them.

New patients come overwhelmingly from local search, so the highest-leverage work is usually making sure you rank for the high-intent terms in your area and that the site converts the click into a booked first visit. Existing patients are an asset most practices under-use: a recall system that automatically reminds people they're due, and a reactivation flow that wins back the ones who drifted, fills chairs that would otherwise sit empty — without anyone at the desk having to remember to call.

On patient data, we build with privacy front of mind: secure forms, sensible data handling, and care about what gets stored where. To be precise — we're a web and software firm, not a HIPAA compliance certifier. The systems that store protected health information (your practice-management software, your CRM) are where a Business Associate Agreement and formal compliance live. We configure those tools to keep PHI out of places it shouldn't be, and we'll tell you plainly where a signed BAA or a compliance specialist is the right call rather than pretend a website fixes that for you.

## How an engagement works

We start with where new patients come from and where the schedule leaks, not with a design. A typical engagement runs Discover → Design → Build → Grow:

1. **Discover.** We map your new-patient sources, your no-show and recall picture, what your front desk is buried under, and which services you want to grow.
2. **Design.** We design the site and booking flow around new-patient conversion — the journey is decided before it's built.
3. **Build.** We build the site, wire the CRM recall and reminder automation, integrate with your scheduling and practice-management tools over their official interfaces, and stand up call handling — tested before launch.
4. **Grow.** Local SEO and ongoing improvement compound new-patient flow over the following months. Every account stays in your name; you own the site, the data, and the patient relationships.

- **We don't provide clinical, legal, or compliance certification.** We build and configure the digital systems; formal HIPAA compliance and any signed BAA sit with the platforms and specialists who own that responsibility.
